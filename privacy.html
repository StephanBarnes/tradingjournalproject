Your information is stored securely on encrypted servers. I use best practices to prevent unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure of your data.

4. Third-Party Services

I do not sell or rent your data. Some third-party services (e.g., analytics, cloud hosting) may temporarily access anonymised data to help maintain the platform.

This site uses cookies for login sessions, analytics, and performance enhancements. For more information, read our Cookies Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your trading data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all data at any time.

7. Your Rights

In line with GDPR and data protection laws, you have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. You may also object to data processing where applicable.
