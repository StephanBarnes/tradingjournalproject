Frequently Asked Questions

What is Trade Journal and who is it for?

Trade Journal is a web-based tool I created to help forex, crypto, and indices traders track, analyse, and improve their performance. Whether you're a beginner or full-time trader, it's designed to support your growth.

Is it free to use?

Yes, the core features are completely free. I plan to introduce optional premium tools later — but I’ll always keep the essentials accessible to everyone.

Do I need to install anything?

Nope — it runs entirely in your browser. You can access it from your desktop, laptop, or phone.

Can I import trades from a CSV file?

Absolutely. You can upload a CSV and the system will automatically process and display your trades in the dashboard.

How does the AI feature work?

The AI analyses your trade history and provides predictive insights based on patterns it detects. It’s built using TensorFlow.js and improves as more data is added.

Is my data secure?

Yes — all trade data is stored securely and linked only to your user account. I don't share or sell user data. Your privacy is a top priority.

I found a bug or have a feature idea — what should I do?

I’d love to hear about it! Just head to the Contact Us page and drop me a message.
