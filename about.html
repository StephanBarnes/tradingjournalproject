About Trade Journal

I created Trade Journal with the goal of simplifying trade tracking. My mission is to empower traders with clarity, insight, and smart tools for decision-making.

Our Vision

Trade Journal exists to redefine how modern traders approach analysis and learning. I believe that every trader, whether beginner or experienced, deserves access to clean, actionable data about their performance without overcomplication.

What Makes Us Different

  • I focus on real usability — no bloated features or unnecessary distractions.
  • The dashboard is designed to be intuitive and mobile-friendly.
  • I integrated AI-powered tools to help traders review past data and make future decisions with confidence.
  • Every part of the app is made with simplicity, speed, and trader psychology in mind.

Who This Is For

This platform is for forex, crypto, and indices traders who are tired of manually tracking or losing sight of their edge. Whether you're a student balancing studies with trading, or a full-time trader refining your strategy, Trade Journal is built to support your growth.

Behind the Project

I'm Stephan, a trader and student with a passion for systems that drive real growth. I created Trade Journal as part of my university final year project — but it's grown far beyond that. I'm committed to improving and expanding the features with real feedback from users.

Continuous Development

This isn’t a one-time build. I’ve structured the project to grow. That includes:

  • Adding deeper analytics and visual insights
  • Expanding AI capabilities for predictive feedback
  • Building a community of traders who want to learn, refine, and win together

Let’s Grow Together

Trade Journal is more than a tool — it’s a commitment. I’m constantly listening to traders, learning from the data, and optimising the platform to help users succeed with confidence. Thank you for being part of this journey.
